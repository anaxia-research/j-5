# Only Maxwell Molecules Contract the Wasserstein Distance: Inverse-Power Forces, Hard Spheres and the Landau Equation

Deep Bhattacharjee

Tanaka's theorem says that two Boltzmann solutions never move apart in the quadratic Wasserstein distance when the molecules are Maxwellian. This paper proves that this fails for every other power law |v − v*|^γ, γ > −3, with bounded angular kernel, for every other inverse-power force without angular cutoff, and for the Landau equation with every non-Maxwellian power law, the Coulomb case included, answering Villani's question for these interactions. For γ > 32.2 even the distance to the Maxwellian can increase; near the Maxwellian the question is reduced to the sign of an explicit quadratic form.
